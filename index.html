Application Process

How to apply for housing.

Our process is straightforward. We want to remove barriers, not create them. Here's what to expect from first contact to move-in.

1

Reach Out

Contact us by email or phone to express your interest. You can also be referred by a case manager, probation officer, court, or social services agency.

2

Intake Screening

We'll have a brief conversation to understand your situation, release date, and needs. This is not an interview — it's a conversation to see how we can help.

3

Application & Review

Complete a short application. We review all submissions and will contact you with a decision or next steps within a reasonable timeframe based on availability.

4

Program Orientation

Approved residents complete an orientation covering house expectations, support services, and the path toward stable independent housing.

5

Move In

Welcome home. Your journey toward stability, employment, and community starts here.

Ready to take the first step? Contact us today to start your application or ask questions about availability.

Who Qualifies

Eligibility requirements.

  • ✓ Justice-involved adult (18+) recently released or preparing for release from incarceration
  • ✓ No stable housing plan upon release from a correctional facility
  • ✓ Willingness to participate in program structure, case planning, and house rules
  • ✓ Located in or returning to Macon-Bibb County, Georgia
  • ✓ Commitment to seeking employment or participating in workforce training
  • ✓ Must not pose a direct safety risk to other residents (assessed individually)

Priority Populations

While we serve all eligible justice-involved adults, we give priority placement to:


  • ★ Justice-involved veterans
  • ★ Adults at moderate-to-high recidivism risk with no housing plan
  • ★ Individuals referred through Georgia DCS Reentry Partnership Housing program

Not sure if you qualify? Contact us anyway. We'll do our best to connect you with the right resources even if we can't house you directly.

For Case Managers & Partners

Refer a client to us.
