The Structure

Structure is the point.

This isn't just a roof. It's a program with expectations — because that's what keeps people from going back.

1

Clear house rules, from day one. You know exactly what's expected before you ever move in. No surprises, no guessing.

2

Regular check-ins. Staff knows your name, your plan, and where you stand on it.

3

Everybody contributes. You're working, in job training, or actively hunting. Nobody's sitting still here.

4

Respect the house and the people in it. Clean, sober, accountable. That's the deal, for everyone.

Plain talk: if structure sounds like a lot, this probably isn't your place yet. If it sounds like exactly what you need — keep reading.

Who It's For

Built for people coming home.

Reborn Ready Housing serves justice-involved adults returning to Macon-Bibb County. Here's who fits:

  • Justice-involved adults, 18 and up
  • Coming home to Macon-Bibb County
  • No stable housing plan in place
  • Ready to work a program — employed, in training, or job hunting
  • Veterans get priority placement

One thing, said plainly: you have to want it. We bring the bed and the structure. You bring the effort.

Referrals

For case managers, courts & partners.

Work with someone coming home to Macon-Bibb who needs structured housing? Send them our way.

What to send

  • Their name and a way to reach them
  • Expected release date, if known
  • What they're walking into — supervision status, immediate needs

Where to send it

Email info@rebornreadyhousing.org with “Referral” in the subject line. We'll take it from there.
